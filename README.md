## Introduction

This dashboard was created for **Job Seekers, Job Transitioners, and Job Swappers** to slove a common problems: information about the data job market is scattered and hard to grasp. using a *real-world dataset of 2024 data science job posting* (including title, salaries, and locations), this project provides a single,easy-to-use interface to explore market trends and compensations. 

## Skill Showcased

- **Date Transformation (ETL) with Power Query:**
Cleaned, Shaped, and Prepared raw data for analysis by handling blanks, Chaning data types, and Creating new columns.

- **🧮 Implicit Measures**: Built-in aggregations such as Sum, Average, and Count.

- **📊 Core Charts & Data Visualization**: Bar charts, column charts, line charts, and donut charts.

- **🌍 Geospatial Analysis**: Mapping job opportunities across locations.

- **🎯 KPI Indicators & Cards**: Highlighting job counts, average salaries, and key metrics.

- **📋 Tables & Matrix Visuals**: Displaying detailed job postings and salary comparisons.

- **🎨 Dashboard Design & Layout**: Creating a clean, intuitive, and visually appealing dashboard.

- **🔄 Iterative Reporting**: Refining visuals and reports through testing and improvements.
        
  - **Slicers:** Added interactive slicers to filter job postings by job title, location, experience level, and salary range, enabling users to explore relevant job market insights.

  - **Buttons & Bookmarks:** Used buttons and bookmarks to enable smooth navigation between report pages, switch dashboard views, and improve the overall user experience.

  - **Drill-Through:** Implemented drill-through functionality to let users navigate from summary visuals to detailed job information for specific job titles, locations, or salary categories.
